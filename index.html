Made for your own setup

Keep the hardware simple. Make the software powerful.

🎛️

Visual button editor

Add emojis, built-in symbols or your own pictures. Give each key a title and a smaller description.

⌨️

Custom actions

Launch installed apps, open websites, send keyboard shortcuts, control music and more.

🔊

Encoder control

Assign clockwise, counterclockwise and press actions. Tune sensitivity and volume steps.

🎨

Personal themes

Dark and light appearance, accent palettes and two-color gradients.

🔌

Automatic reconnection

The Windows companion can reconnect when your paired ESP32 is plugged in again.

↻

Updates & profiles

Keep separate layouts and install validated update packages without losing your settings.

How it works

Your ESP32 sends button events over USB. The Windows companion handles shortcuts, apps and profiles.

1
Connect the hardware3 buttons, an encoder and a classic ESP32 to begin.
2
Upload the firmwareArduino IDE sends the matching device-identification sketch once.
3
Install the companionRun the included Windows setup script and pick your actions.

Built for upgrades

The companion stores your profiles, pairing and uploaded button images separately from the app installation.
